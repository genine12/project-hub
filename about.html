How I work

A few principles that carry across most projects here.

  • Ship, then refine.A working version beats a perfect plan — most of these started rough and were shaped through use.
  • Design with restraint.Fewer colors, fewer decisions, more room for the content to speak.
  • Motion with purpose.Animation should clarify a transition, not decorate it.
  • Keep it documented.Every project gets a PRD or notes — future me always asks why a decision was made.
